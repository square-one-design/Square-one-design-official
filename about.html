About me

Experience You Can Rely On

With over 40 years of experience, I bring a wealth of knowledge to every project, helping customers turn their ideas into clear, practical and achievable plans.

I began my career in architectural practice in 1988, gaining extensive experience in design, drawing and planning. I later completed a degree in Construction Management, further developing my understanding of how projects come together from the initial idea through to completion.

My experience has also given me a good understanding of the people and processes involved in a building project. Over the years, I have developed strong relationships with local professionals, builders, tradespeople, suppliers and council departments.

For customers who may have little experience with building or planning, the process can seem complicated. My aim is to make it as simple and straightforward as possible, providing clear advice and personal support from your initial ideas through to your completed drawings.

All drawings are produced using CAD, making it easy to develop and update your plans as your project takes shape.
